## 1. Name

**CARSON.** Not OxyCarson. Not Carson Overseas.

- Parent firm: **Carson Overseas** — manufacture and export of leather footwear.
- Manufacturing division: **M/s. Shoe Connect**, established **1995**.
- "OxyCarson" was a sub-brand of the firm. Dropped — the `Oxy` prefix cheapens the name.
- "Overseas" dropped — reads freight and trade, not luxury.
- The consumer-facing brand is the single word **CARSON**.

> ⚠️ **OPEN — trademark.** Rights are held for *Carson Overseas* and *OxyCarson*, **not** for
> *Carson* standalone. "Carson" is a common surname and Class 25 (footwear) is crowded.
> Needs a clearance search before money goes into signage, boxes, or ads.
> Fallback lockups: `CARSON 1995`, `CARSON ATELIER`, `HOUSE OF CARSON`, or a stylised
> wordmark registered as a device mark.

> ⚠️ **Existing logo is not reusable.** The Carson Overseas mark — blue globe inside a green
> "C" — is B2B export-trade visual language. It signals freight forwarding, not footwear.
> The consumer brand needs a wholly separate identity. Keep the old mark for the B2B firm.

## 2. Positioning

- **Tier:** Elevated contemporary. Roughly $150–320 / ₹12,000–26,000.
- **Register:** Maison-grade presentation with an identity that is our own.
- **Hard constraint from founder:** *"It should look like a product, not an art project."*
  Beauty in service of the shoe, never in front of it.

## 3. The story — LEAD WITH THIS

**Since 1995.** Three decades manufacturing and exporting leather footwear from Agra to Europe,
the US, the Gulf and Southeast Asia. Carson is that workshop stepping out from behind its clients
and putting its own name on the box.

> **For thirty years, our work left in someone else's box.**

Nearly every new premium sneaker brand is a marketing team that found a factory.
Carson is the factory. That is the whole argument.

## 4. Verified credentials — from the company profile

These are the real assets. Every one is documented, and none requires naming a client.

| Fact | Why it matters on a consumer site |
|---|---|
| **Established 1995** | Thirty years. A date beats any adjective. |
| **Associated with a prominent Italian design house**, seasonal footwear design | The single most valuable line in the document. Italian design + Agra craft. |
| **SA-8000 certified**; ethical and technical audits to international standards | Third-party-verified labour standards. Premium Western buyers care, and almost no competitor at this price can claim it. |
| **Sources its own leather** — Kolkata and Chennai tanneries, nationwide network | Vertical integration. We control the material, not just the assembly. |
| **In-house manufacturing, own finance** | No middle layer. Explains the price directly. |
| **35,000 pairs/month capacity** | Real industrial capability; we will not run out of stock. |
| **₹70+ crore turnover** | Substance. For press and wholesale, not the consumer site. |
| **Own showroom and development facility** | Design happens here, not just assembly. |

**The Italian design house association solves the Agra problem.** The founder's worry was that
Agra reads as low-end. The answer is already in the business: Italian design, Indian craft,
audited to international standards. That is Carson's actual sentence, and it is true.

## 5. Clients — DECISION: do not name them on the consumer site

Documented in the profile. **Export:** bugatti, Geigei S.R.L., PJ Keng, Gulab Group (UAE),
Pioneer Export (HK), Phoenix (USA), Larrie Corporation (Malaysia).
**Domestic:** BATA. Brands pictured: Trotters, Softwalk, Azaleia, Crocodile Taupoli.

> ⚠️ **Discrepancy to resolve.** The founder named Red Chief, Metro and Tata/Zudio verbally;
> the profile names BATA and the export list above. Confirm which relationships are current
> before any of this is used anywhere, including wholesale decks.

**Decision: imply, do not name.** Reasons:

1. **Legal.** Manufacturing agreements routinely carry confidentiality, and using a client's
   trademark to promote your own competing brand invites a cease-and-desist.
2. **Positioning.** The list is mixed-tier. BATA next to a $300 sneaker caps what you can charge.
   A mixed list averages downward.
3. **Power.** "You have probably already worn our work" is stronger than any list — the reader
   fills the gap with the best name they can think of.
4. **It is now unnecessary.** §4 gives three better, zero-risk credentials: since 1995, the
   Italian design association, and SA-8000. Lead with those instead.

The B2B profile is a different document for a different audience. Naming clients there is
normal practice and should continue. The consumer site is not that document.

Approved on-site phrasing:

> Thirty years supplying footwear brands across Europe, the US and Asia.
> You have probably already worn our work.

## 6. Origin — DECISION: stated plainly, exactly once

**Made in Agra, India.** Stated as fact in the craft section and footer. Nowhere else.
No apology, no hedging, no Taj Mahal, no tourism imagery.

Buyers at this price ask where shoes are made, and a hidden answer discovered later damages
far more than a plain one read up front. Confidence is the trick. Pairing it with the Italian
design association (§4) makes it read as sophistication rather than compromise.

## 7. Collection at launch

| Silhouette | Notes |
|---|---|
| Low-top leather sneaker | Founder's intended hero. |
| Boots — Chelsea + lace-up | Highest price anchor, strongest craft story. |
| Loafers | Smart-casual, widens the audience. |

> **Observation worth a decision.** The showroom and factory photography shows the demonstrated
> strength is *formal and smart leather footwear* — oxfords, brogues, derbies, boots — produced
> at scale and to export standard. Sneakers are the crowded category with the most entrenched
> competition; dress-adjacent leather is where thirty years of welted-shoe capability is an
> actual moat. Recommend leading the launch with **loafers and boots**, with the sneaker as the
> third silhouette rather than the hero. Founder's call.

## 8. Construction claims — what we may actually say

Founder-verified:
- **Full-grain leather** on the top of the range
- **Hand-finishing / partial hand-stitching** on the upper
- **Leather sourced from own tannery network** (Kolkata, Chennai) — documented
- **SA-8000 certified production** — documented

**Copy rule.** Say *"hand-finished"* and *"partly hand-stitched"*. Do **not** say *"handmade"*
or *"entirely hand-stitched"* — untrue at scale and indefensible when a customer asks.

> ⚠️ **UNVERIFIED — confirm before it ships:** Goodyear welted? Vegetable-tanned?
> Which Italian design house, and may it be referenced (even unnamed)?
